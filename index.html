How the numbers work

Expected goals (xG)

xG is the chance a shot becomes a goal, judged before the ball is struck. A 0.30 xG chance is scored about three times in ten. Touchline uses its own model, trained on every non-penalty shot in this season's event data.

The model looks at distance and angle to goal, body part, whether it was a first-time shot, a one-on-one or under pressure, how the chance was made (cross, through ball, cutback, set piece), how many defenders stood between the ball and goal, and where the keeper was. Penalties are fixed at 0.76.

To keep it honest, each shot is scored by a version of the model that never saw that shot, so a striker's goals can't teach the model that his chances were easy.

Expected assists (xA)

xA credits the passer with the xG of the shot their pass set up, whether or not it was scored. It rewards creating chances rather than relying on the finisher.

Other definitions

Conversion is non-penalty goals divided by non-penalty shots. Progressive passes and carries move the ball at least 25% closer to the goal and end in the attacking 60% of the pitch; set pieces are excluded. Minutes are counted on a 90-minute clock. Percentiles compare a player with others in the same position who played at least 900 minutes.

Data

Event data from StatsBomb Open Data, which StatsBomb releases free for research and fan analysis. Player and team stats on this site are calculated by Touchline from that event data.

Model check

AUC: how often the model rates a goal above a miss (0.5 is a coin flip). Lower log loss is better.
